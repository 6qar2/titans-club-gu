XII // FAQ

YOUR QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

How much time does membership require each week?

4–6 hours weekly. More during major event weeks.

What are the benefits of joining?

Leadership experience, portfolio projects, mentorship, and access to high-impact campus initiatives.

What does the selection process look like?

Application review, shortlist notification, unit interview, then final onboarding invitation.

Can first-year students apply?

Yes. Any current Galala University student can apply if they meet commitment and conduct standards.

What are the membership rules?

Attend required sessions, complete tasks on time, and represent the club with respect on and off campus.

Is there a membership fee?

No. Membership is free. Any required materials or event costs are communicated in advance and kept minimal.

How can I contact a specific committee?

Each unit has a dedicated contact link on the Team page. You can also reach the general inbox at thetitansgu@gmail.com.
