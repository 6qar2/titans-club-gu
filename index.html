XI // FAQ

YOUR QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

How much time does membership require each week?

Most Titans commit 4–6 hours weekly, with higher involvement during major event weeks.

What are the benefits of joining?

Hands-on leadership experience, portfolio-building projects, mentorship, and access to high-impact campus initiatives.

What does the selection process look like?

Application review, shortlist notification, unit interview, and final onboarding invitation.

Can first-year students apply?

Yes. All current Galala University students can apply as long as they meet commitment and conduct standards.

What are the membership rules?

Members must follow attendance requirements, complete assigned tasks on time, and represent the club respectfully on and off campus.
