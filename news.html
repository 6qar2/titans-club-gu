ANNOUNCEMENT

TITANS LEADERSHIP ANNOUNCED

The command structure is set. Meet the Titans leadership team that will drive the club's vision forward.

The wait is over. After weeks of interviews, evaluations, and internal deliberation, the Titans Club has finalized its 2026 command structure. At the helm is President Hesham Yasser, whose vision for an authentic, dominant, and elite club has set the tone for every decision since day one. He is flanked by Advisor Aly, whose steady hand ensures the club stays grounded while pushing boundaries.

Directly under the command structure are two directors who will define the club's public face: Beshoy as Events Director, responsible for every activation, festival, and on-ground experience the Titans deliver; and Karim Elsherbini as Media and Branding Director, the creative force behind the club's identity across Instagram, TikTok, and every visual asset the world sees.

"This isn't just a leadership lineup — it's a statement," said Hesham Yasser. "Every department head was chosen because they embody what it means to be a Titan. Discipline in execution. Dominance in presence. Authenticity in everything we create."

The announcement was made at a closed-door induction ceremony at Galala University, attended by founding members, department leads, and representatives from partner organizations. The leadership team will officially assume their roles ahead of the September inauguration festival.

AUGUST 2026
MILESTONE

900+ FOLLOWERS ON INSTAGRAM

The Titans Club reaches 900+ followers and counting.

In less than three months since its official launch, the Titans Club Instagram account has crossed the 900-follower mark — a milestone that reflects more than just numbers. It signals a growing movement of students at Galala University who want more than clubs. They want identity, discipline, and a shared mission.

The growth has been organic, driven by a content strategy that blends behind-the-scenes looks at preparation, student testimonials, event highlights, and the club's signature visual style — dark, sharp, and unapologetically bold. The account has become the primary touchpoint for applicants curious about what the Titans actually represent.

"We don't chase trends," said Karim Elsherbini, Media and Branding Director. "We build a brand that feels like a culture. When people follow us, they're not following a student account — they're aligning with something bigger."

With the inauguration festival approaching in September, the club expects to hit 1,000 followers before the first major campus activation. The team is already planning a special content series to mark the occasion.

AUGUST 2026
BRAND

WE WILL LEAD GALALA

The new brand identity drops. Authentic. Dominant. Elite.

The Titans Club has unveiled its complete brand identity system — a visual language built to command attention and communicate authority without saying a word. The system spans logo usage, color protocols, typography standards, social media templates, and event collateral guidelines.

At the center of the identity is the new emblem: a circular mark that balances weight and precision, designed to work at 16 pixels on a phone screen and 16 feet on a campus banner. The color palette draws from deep crimson, metallic gold, and absolute black — colors chosen to signal strength, value, and control.

"Branding isn't just about looking good," said Karim Elsherbini. "It's about making sure every piece of content, every poster, every Instagram story feels like it came from the same source. Consistency builds trust. Trust builds culture."

The brand guidelines are now being distributed to all department leads and will be enforced across every Titans touchpoint moving forward. The full style guide will be available to members through the Members Portal.

AUGUST 2026
EVENT

INAUGURATION FESTIVAL

Full details drop soon. Get ready for the biggest campus activation of the year.

Mark your calendars. The Titans Club Inauguration Festival is coming to Galala University this September, and it will be the largest single-day activation the campus has ever seen. The event is being planned as a full-scale experience — competitive, cultural, and unmissable.

Attendees can expect live performances, team challenges, interactive zones, guest speakers, and the official public reveal of the club's command structure. The festival will also serve as the primary recruitment drive for the 2026 intake, with on-site application stations and immediate-screening interviews for walk-in candidates.

Beshoy, Events Director, confirmed that the event will span the entire campus grounds. "We're not doing a small hall event. This is a takeover. Every corner of Galala University will feel the Titans presence on that day."

Sponsorship packages for the festival are available at Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers. Partners interested in activation slots should contact the club via the Partners page. Full event details, timing, and ticketing information will be released in the coming weeks.
